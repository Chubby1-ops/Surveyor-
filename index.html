Surveyor Guide

Everything users need to know.

Learn how to create, customise, pay with credits, download, share and reuse results.

01

Start in either direction

I have something is for an existing number, username, link, email, Wi-Fi detail, location, contact, payment link, event, meeting, media link, text or multiple links. Paste it, confirm the type and select results.

I want to create is for choosing the required result first. Search for the output, select it, then enter the requested information.

02

Input types and detection

Use Auto for automatic recognition, or manually choose Phone, Messaging, Social, URL, Email, Wi-Fi, Location, Contact, Payment, Event, Meeting, Media, Text or Multiple. Manual selection always overrides detection.

03

Select one, several or all

Tap the circular control beside every required output. Select one output, several compatible outputs or use Select all. The review screen shows the chosen items and total cost before generation.

04

QR codes

Single-purpose QR codes contain the direct data or destination. A website QR opens the website; a Wi-Fi QR contains network connection data. Completed QR codes can be downloaded as high-resolution PNG files with white padding.

05

Pages, cards and combined links

Page-style outputs present information or multiple destinations in one place. Frontend-only pages keep their information inside the URL hash, so the URL can be longer than a normal direct link. The result screen displays a short readable label while Copy and Share use the complete URL.

07

Download, open, copy and share

Links can be copied, opened and shared. QR codes download as PNG. Contact data downloads as VCF where applicable, calendar data as ICS and notes as TXT. Generated direct links go to their intended destination, not back through Telegram unless Telegram was selected.

08

Recent results and editing

Recent results are kept locally for free reuse. Editing an existing result costs 1 credit only after a successful update. Cancelling an edit leaves the original result unchanged and costs nothing.

09

Privacy and Telegram

Generator entries are processed locally in the browser. This frontend preview stores lightweight state locally. Secure cross-device balances, account identity and referral validation require server-side verification of Telegram launch data.

10

Moving around

Swipe left or right on the page to move through Home, Create, Explore and Guide. Swiping horizontal chips or category rails moves that list instead of changing the main view.
